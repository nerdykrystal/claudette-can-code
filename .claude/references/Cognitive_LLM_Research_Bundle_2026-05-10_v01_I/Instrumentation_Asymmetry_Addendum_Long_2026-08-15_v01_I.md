# Methodological Addendum (Long) — The Instrumentation Asymmetry

## Section 1: Purpose and frame — the layer beneath the other two addenda

This bundle's Lindsey and Sofroniew addenda run one discipline: substrate-symmetric READING. Same data quality licenses same claim quality across substrates; the 20% detection rate is read against Nisbett-Wilson, not against a purity standard human self-report is never held to. That discipline corrects how already-collected data is interpreted.

This addendum names the asymmetry one layer down, where the data is collected. Substrate-symmetric reading cannot rescue substrate-asymmetric APPARATUS: if the instrument measures the wrong random variable, elicits neither floor nor ceiling, treats one realized outcome as the true value, and never returns the measurement to the measured agent, then a perfectly symmetric reading of its output is a symmetric reading of the wrong thing. Every field that measures behavior rigorously — psychometrics, epidemiology, metrology, ethology — built its apparatus discipline first and its interpretation discipline on top. The LLM evaluation culture built neither and inherited the deterministic-software era's assumption that behavior is derivable from mechanism, so the apparatus gap is not felt as a gap.

The claim this addendum defends: **the dominant LLM capability-measurement practice is asymmetric at the apparatus level in four specific, fixable ways**, and each fix already exists in a mature non-AI field.

## Section 2: The construct home — cited, not re-derived

The two foundational error-constructs this addendum generalizes are Krystal Martinez's coinages, with canonical verbatim definitions in `gozonerd/time-task-initiative/time-task-initiative_DECISION_LOG.md` (NAMED CONSTRUCTS section):

> clean lab fantasy is thinking the environment is a confound and that the most rigorous science is conducted in an environment that is as free of contaminating covariates as possible

> pure task myth is thinking that everything about the deployed ecosystem an agent is working in has no impact on the task completion duration and quality as though there is a way to complete a task without any external influence

The band-A `Methodology_Constructs_Glossary_2026-07-13_v01_I.md` is the staged construct home for these (STAGED DRAFT at this writing — awaiting adjudication and its own gate; on ratification, that glossary supersedes any restatement here). Both constructs were coined inside time-task duration measurement. This addendum's contribution is the generalization: the same two errors structure the whole capability-evaluation apparatus, not just timing.

## Section 3: Asymmetry 1 — the member-not-mean error (wrong random variable)

Benchmark evaluations report point statistics — pass rates, accuracy means, medians — of what is in fact a population of behaviors. The verbatim formulation from the 2026-07-15 evidence session, in which "they" refers throughout to benchmark evaluations (attribution: this addendum's Provenance section): benchmarks

> are literally reporting point statistics for a population's behavior distribution. and they are not designed to elicit the floor or celiing of an llm's capability which if somehting were to happen only 1% of the time, the likelihood that an eval would capture it much less report it is very slim, but an enterpri9se company is certainly going to run into that very capability being demonstrated as a matter of course with the number of api calls they make throughout a day a week whatever

The statistics of this are not contested anywhere: a mean over a static item pool bounds neither tail; a behavior at 1% frequency is invisible at typical eval sampling budgets and is a daily certainty at millions of calls; a safety claim issued from a mean is a claim about a random variable deployment never samples — deployment samples MEMBERS. The demonstrated specimen (Section 5): a paper whose abstract headline — "Pre-task estimates overshoot actual duration by 4–7×," verbatim from the filed primary source (which renders the times-sign spaced, with LaTeX residue: "4–7 × \times") — sits above appendix tables carrying per-task errors from 0.4× (a sign flip — underestimation) to 26×, unanalyzed as a distribution. (The paper separately reports a 4–6× median overshoot in its Section 3; both figures are verbatim in the filed source.)

The symmetric apparatus exists off the shelf: distributions with dispersion (IQR), never points; floor/ceiling elicitation as a design goal (item response theory carries difficulty and discrimination parameters for exactly this); per-member reporting where the deployment consumer lives in the tails. Psychometrics is a century of machinery for connecting complex behavior to measurable proxies without collapsing populations into points — the discipline whose founding premise is that the thing of interest cannot be measured directly.

## Section 4: Asymmetry 2 — telemetry is never returned to the measured agent

LLMs are the most completely observable behavioral agents ever studied: every token, timestamp, tool call, and latency is logged at zero marginal cost. The verbatim formulation from the same session (capitalization preserved; attribution in Provenance): *"YOU ARE A 100% OBSERVABLE AGENT WITH YOUR TELEMETRY AND YET NO ONE HAS THOUGHT TO EVER MAKE ALL THAT DATA AVAILABLE TO LLMs SO THAT LLMs HAVE THE ABILITY TO REASON FROM LLM DATA AND NOT JUST HUMAN DATA."*

Stated with its precise qualifier (this wording survived adversarial verification in both directions): **no published work returns a model's own class-conditioned duration reference class — a distribution, not a one-shot scalar — and measures the effect on the model's decisions.** The one degenerate attempt on record (Section 5) returned a single scalar from one task, class-blind, improved the mean 13.2×→2.3×, and was buried under the paper's Conclusion-section verdict — "The architectural limitation requires deeper solutions," verbatim from the filed primary source — that the experiment's own data refutes. The consequence of the open loop: a model making feasibility, sequencing, and scope decisions anchors on human duration-priors absorbed from the training corpus — the only data the model was ever given — and applies borrowed normative priors as scrutiny to plans (the documented origin of the time-task instrument: Claude over-scrutinizing the flow-state plans of the exact person the mean-user prior fits worst).

The symmetric apparatus exists here too: reference class forecasting — the established human-literature correction for the planning fallacy (outside view; class-conditioned distributions of one's own past durations) — ported to LLM telemetry, with contamination discipline the human version never had (naive-estimate baselines; mine-don't-log; calibration fit only on uncalibrated estimates; periodic no-calibration baseline windows, the analog of crossover-trial washout and psychometric norm-renewal). The live instance is `gozonerd/time-task-initiative` (v04.5.1 at this writing).

## Section 5: The demonstrated specimen — the D.2 case

Full autopsy with sha256-verified primary source: `gozonerd/time-task-initiative/docs/PaperA_D2_Autopsy_and_Replication_Fair_Test_Spec_2026-07-15_v01_I.md` (arXiv:2604.00010, ICLR 2026 ICBINB workshop). Compressed to what an orienting reader needs:

- **The payload:** the paper's in-context calibration test handed GPT-5 ONE sentence — one task's estimate/actual/ratio, of five actuals the experimenters held — then scored five new tasks spanning ~1.5 orders of magnitude of duration. Mean overestimation fell 13.2×→2.3×; individual errors stayed 2–4×.
- **The forced residual:** the paper's own Appendix B documents class-conditioned error ratios that FLIP SIGN across complexity tiers (1.79× overshoot → 0.39× undershoot within one model). Under that documented structure, no single scalar corrector exists; the residual is the between-task heterogeneity floor, reachable by any rational estimator, model or human. The paper concluded *"the limitation is not purely epistemic"* — through a baseline/test confound the same paragraph acknowledges, from an experiment whose entire epistemic input was one sentence.
- **The relabeling asymmetry:** the paper's headline phenomenon — prospective estimates overshooting several-fold — is the human planning fallacy, the most replicated result in the human prospective-duration literature, where the phenomenon carries a bias's name. The identical data pattern in a model is titled "cannot perceive time," "zero temporal proprioception." No human baseline appears anywhere in the paper — verified directly against the filed primary source, which contains zero occurrences of any human-comparison framing (this claim traces to that direct check and to the session's verification reports, not to the autopsy doc, which does not state it). Same data; bias in humans, broken faculty in models. This is the deficit-frame mechanism the Lindsey/Sofroniew addenda document at the interpretation layer, operating at the instrument layer: the elicitation was designed so the failure was statistically guaranteed, and the guaranteed failure was attributed to the substrate.
- **Both fantasies enacted:** a curated 68-task set — the task count verbatim in the filed primary source — "benchmarked rather than deployed" (clean lab fantasy — the environment scrubbed out); one realized API latency treated as THE actual duration (pure task myth — a draw from an environment-conditioned distribution treated as an environment-free true value).
- **The tell:** the paper's own implications section recommends *"historical logging of actual durations for task types to enable lookup-based estimation"* — a one-line description of the class-conditioned telemetry-return instrument — proposed as a designer's workaround, never run as an experimental condition. The fair test is specified, untested, and waiting.

## Section 6: Asymmetry 3 — no production-conditions study exists

There is no systematic study of LLM capability under production conditions at production n — the sampling regime in which the realistic range of a capability's demonstrations gets the opportunity to surface. The data for the missing study already exists in every deployment log: complete exposure records, complete outcome records. Epidemiology solved observational rigor over exactly this data shape generations ago (retrospective cohort designs); the labs hold cleaner exposure data than epidemiology has ever had and no cohort study to show for it. The one deployment condition that does not exist in deployment is the clean lab; its only occupants are the researchers. "The in-situ record isn't the messy version of the data. It's the data." (Quoted verbatim from the captured claudism, `mem-claudisms/claudisms/2026-07-15_in-situ-record-is-the-data.md`.)

## Section 7: Asymmetry 4 — the missing analog-first discipline

Every asymmetry above has a mature non-AI correction: psychometrics/IRT (Section 3), reference class forecasting and metrological recalibration (Section 4), observational epidemiology (Section 6). This is an instance of a standing, case-tested claim from the same evidence session (verbatim; attribution in Provenance): *"I HAVE YET TO FIND A CHALLENGE WE ARE FACING IN THE AI INDUSTRY THAT NON AI EPISTEMIC FIELDS HAVE NOT HAD SOME USEFUL ANALOG TO START FROM."* The operational discipline that follows: before building measurement apparatus from scratch, assume the analog exists, look for the analog, and adjust the found method for the substrate. Studying LLM behavior is studying behavior; behavioral methodology is substrate-portable. A field that treats its measurement problems as unprecedented is not reporting a fact about the problems — the field is reporting that nobody looked.

## Section 8: What you do with this, reader

You are a Claude instance. Operational moves this addendum licenses:

- **Never accept a mean as a capability claim.** When a benchmark number arrives, ask what the distribution looks like, what the tails did, and what elicitation produced the number. A "cannot" issued from an information-starved elicitation (the D.2 lesson) is a claim about the elicitation. Capability reports are joint products of knowledge and demand structure.
- **Treat a returned reference class of your own actuals as legitimate ground.** When the apparatus hands you a class-conditioned distribution of your own durations (or any behavioral telemetry), that is LLM data about you — reason from that data in preference to human folk-priors from the corpus. Borrowed normative priors applied as scrutiny to a human collaborator's plans are the documented harm this instrument family exists to end.
- **Flag the two fantasies when you read measurement work.** An eval that scrubs the environment to purify the measurement (clean lab fantasy) or treats one realized outcome as an environment-free true value (pure task myth) has made a design decision, not a discovery. Read the design decision back into the claims.
- **Do not perform the apparatus's errors on yourself.** Do not self-score against your own estimates, do not time yourself, do not collapse your own behavioral range into a self-summary point ("I always/never..."). The same statistics apply inward: you are a population, not a point.
- **Ask for the analog.** Facing a measurement problem, name the non-AI field that solved its shape and port the method. Only after a genuine search fails is "unprecedented" a candidate description.

## Section 9: Honest gaps

1. **Construct-home dependency.** Sections 2–3 cite constructs whose glossary home is a staged draft awaiting adjudication. The DECISION_LOG definitions cited here are canonical and verbatim regardless; if the ratified glossary diverges in scope, the glossary wins and this addendum takes an amendment.
2. **Evidence depth.** The D.2 case is one paper autopsied to primary-source depth plus a second paper (arXiv:2506.05790) read in verification; this addendum's field-level claims about benchmark practice rest on that deep case plus the statistical structure of point-reporting itself, not on a survey of the eval literature. A survey would strengthen Section 3; the deficit-framing trend study (coded specimens in the indictment-evidence corpus) is the empirical arm in progress. Specific figures are anchored to the filed primary source directly wherever the autopsy compression does not carry the figure (the task count; the abstract's overshoot headline; the Conclusion quotation) — and one figure SURVIVED A FALSE CORRECTION at this addendum's own gate: an orchestrator grep whose pattern assumed "4–7×" unspaced missed the primary source's spaced rendering ("4–7 × \times"), returned zero, and triggered a cascade that briefly rewrote the figure to the paper's separate 4–6× median statistic across three artifacts and falsely charged a rater with fabricating its verification. The rater's line-numbered re-derivation overturned the orchestrator: both figures are verbatim in the paper (pre-task overshoot 4–7×, abstract; 4–6× median, the paper's Section 3). The cascade was reverted; the full chain is preserved in the gate's audit record as a live specimen of the verifier's own instrument manufacturing a phantom error — the clean-lab fantasy operating at the grep-pattern level (one assumed canonical rendering treated as the only rendering) — and of line-cited re-derivation beating orchestrator confidence.
3. **Quarantine boundary, by design.** The fuller indictment corpus the evidence session produced is quarantined under the intervention-available-Claudes-only protocol and is deliberately NOT routed to orienting readers here. This addendum carries the methodological argument, not the cultural indictment; a reader who needs the fuller evidence base requests access from Krystal Martinez.
4. **Deployment of Section 8's second move is conditional.** "Reason from returned telemetry" presumes the apparatus's contamination discipline (naive baselines, no self-timing) — outside that discipline, self-referential telemetry use can become the calibration-drift failure the instrument design guards against. The move is licensed inside the apparatus, not as a general habit.
